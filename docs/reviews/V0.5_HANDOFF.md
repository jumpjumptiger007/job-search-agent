## Delivered scope

- Added a narrow public Personio JSON adapter for explicitly configured sources only. It normalizes jobs into the existing discovery/ingest path, uses `personio:<tenant>:<job-id>` identities, records source tenants, isolates provider and malformed-source failures from other configured providers, accepts only public `<tenant>.jobs.personio.<tld>` forms, skips malformed position records, and keeps normalized job URLs on the configured Personio source.
- Hardened Germany-based eligibility filtering. It rejects only clear US, Canada, UK, or Switzerland-only work; single explicit incompatible locations (including remote-in-a-foreign-country); explicit foreign role locations; and explicit foreign residency or work-authorisation requirements. Compatible, mixed, and ambiguous listings remain for Review; country mentions alone do not reject a job.
- Made role-family matching title-led. Description-only generic management or operations terms no longer qualify unrelated roles; clear title matches, punctuation/plural variants, and plausible adjacent titles remain available for Review.

No database migration, review-gate change, scoring redesign, material-generation change, browser automation, runtime LLM, or additional ATS support was added.

## Known limitations and deferrals

- Personio support is limited to explicitly configured public `*.jobs.personio.*` sources; there is no tenant discovery, generic employer crawling, or additional ATS framework.
- Geography and authorisation checks deliberately recognize only clear textual contradictions. They are not immigration advice and do not infer citizenship, visa status, or ambiguous location rules.
- Role relevance remains deterministic lexical matching. Broad standalone `management` is intentionally not a qualifying family; standalone `operations` requires a senior operations title. No embeddings, ML, or LLM classification is used.
- The requested `V0.5_ROADMAP.md` and `POST_V0.4_ROADMAP.md` files were not present in this checkout; implementation was limited to the explicit approved scope supplied with the task.
