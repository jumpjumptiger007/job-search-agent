## Delivered scope

- Removes the bare `jd_hash` identity fallback. Ingestion now preserves the released URL-first lookup, followed by external ID and company/title/location matching only.
- Makes new-job creation, its first `job_sources` row, and its discovery audit row one SQLite transaction. A failure inserting the initial source rolls back the new job and sequence update.
- Adds V0.6.5 regressions for identical normalized descriptions across independent listings, URL rediscovery with changed metadata, source-insert rollback, and canonical/source ownership.
