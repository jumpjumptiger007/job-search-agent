## Controlled Germany E2E lifecycle

The fixture contained one recent, substantive, hybrid Berlin role for a fictional German technology employer and no real person's data. An injected `CareerOpsTransport` served it only for the explicit non-live Personio hostname, with zero fixture-network requests. The controlled record passed through the pinned Career Ops Personio provider via `discoverPersonio(source, company, limit, transport)`, project normalization, Germany eligibility, and the unchanged private preference matcher. The normalized record then entered only through `recordSource()` and `ingest()`; no job row was inserted or updated directly.
